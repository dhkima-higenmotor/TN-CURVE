Caution

This curve is generated from a simplified model. It does not account for:

  • Magnetic saturation
  • Temperature effects
  • Motor efficiency
  • Drive efficiency
  • Bearing efficiency

Gearbox efficiency is treated as a constant. Because of these simplifications, the result is approximate and should be verified by physical testing to obtain accurate values.
